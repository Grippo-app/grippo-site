# Grippo: make every rep count

Create a distinctive, polished product site grounded in the neighboring mobile application's actual brand and capabilities. The user grants creative direction and requests substantial product research.

## Direction

Performance editorial: substantial graphite, warm white, restrained copper-orange, oversized Manrope, generous negative space, hairline grids, original app illustrations and anatomical artwork. Vary composition between a sculptural hero, an interactive progress demonstration, a real app explorer, and an editorial closing statement. Avoid invented social proof and label demonstration data.

## Boundaries

Keep the static Vite/TypeScript stack, three locales, store routing/analytics, mobile CTA, and policy content/navigation. No new dependencies, backend, or deployment changes. Copy approved assets from mobile without changing its files. Implement in three verifiable slices, then independent review and browser QA.

## Sequence

1. Brand foundations, navigation and hero; build and inspect.
2. Product story, progress interaction and actual app showcase; type-check, test and inspect.
3. Responsive layout, locale copy and accessibility polish; complete checks, independent review and browser QA at phone/tablet/desktop widths.

## Acceptance

- Recognizable original visual identity, strong mobile and desktop compositions.
- Visitors can explore actual app screens and illustrative analytics, then follow valid store links.
- All new text is available in English, Ukrainian and Russian.
- Existing policies and download behavior work; reduced motion and keyboard interaction are supported.
- Existing test suite, TypeScript and production build pass; no horizontal overflow at representative widths.
