Push Notifications

The Application uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a service provided by Google, to deliver push notifications to your device. These notifications may include reminders to start a scheduled workout, prompts to log your current body weight, and alerts about an unfinished workout session left open in the Application.

To send notifications, FCM assigns your device a registration token — a pseudonymous identifier that does not directly identify you as a person. This token is stored on Google's servers and used solely to route notifications to your device. No health or fitness data from the Application is included in or transmitted through this token.

You may disable push notifications at any time through your device's system settings (iOS: Settings → Notifications → Grippo; Android: Settings → Apps → Grippo → Notifications). Disabling notifications does not affect your ability to use the Application or access your data.

Account Management

Follow the steps below to permanently delete your Grippo account. After confirmation, all data associated with the account is removed and cannot be restored.

1

Open your profile from the home screen

From the start screen, tap the user icon in the top-right corner.

2

Go to Settings

In your profile, find the Settings section and tap it to open account options.

3

Delete the account

In settings, tap "Delete account" and confirm. This permanently removes all data.

Important: deletion is irreversible. Activity history, settings, and related information are removed forever.
